What's on, city by city

here.events lists what's on at the arenas, stadiums, theatres, clubs and music venues of 33 cities and neighbourhoods, with the home teams' schedules beside them. Every site is rebuilt each day from the venues' own calendars, the ticket sellers and the leagues' schedules, and each has a list and a month view, a calendar feed for your phone and a widget to embed on your own site. There is nothing to sign up for.
