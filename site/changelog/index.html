Changelog

Last updated November 24, 2026

What has changed on the here.events calendars: cities launched, venues and sources added, sites merged or retired, and how the listings are checked. The listings themselves are rebuilt every day; How It’s Made explains how.

November 2026

  • Nov 24Atlanta: Smith’s Olde Bar is read from its own calendar as well as Ticketmaster, adding about 30 shows.
  • Nov 24Everett: The Muse Whiskey & Coffee is read from its own events calendar. Restaurant and bar calendars on Popmenu can now be read on any here.events site.
  • Nov 16Every calendar gets a How It’s Made page and shows the date of its last daily build. This changelog starts.
  • Nov 14The here.events home page lists what is on, city by city, refreshed daily.
  • Nov 10Seattle Seawolves: schedule and news are read from the club’s new site.
  • Nov 7Venue link audit across 26 calendars: hijacked, dead and wrong venue links replaced. A link whose page has turned to spam is now blocked, and every venue link is re-audited monthly. Venues show their own mark in place of a ticket platform’s.
  • Nov 5Add To My Calendar keeps the filter you had on. A venue renamed on Ticketmaster keeps its shows.
  • Nov 4Venues added from their DICE pages: 38 in Miami, 18 in Chicago, 7 in Austin, 6 in Seattle, 5 in Las Vegas, 4 in San Diego.
  • Nov 3Chop Shop (Chicago) and Sunset Tavern (Seattle) are read from their own DICE pages, with Ticketmaster behind.
  • Nov 2The Burien calendar is retired.
